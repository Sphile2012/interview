Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2024

1. Who We Are

Blade & Bone Barbershop, Waterloo, Durban. Contact: 082 356 2239 • poomeigh503@gmail.com.

2. What We Collect

Information you provide

  • Name, email and phone when booking
  • Notes or preferences during booking
  • Messages via our contact form

Automatically collected

  • Session data stored in your browser (localStorage) to maintain your booking flow
  • Basic browser and device type for site improvement

3. How We Use It

  • To manage and confirm your appointment
  • To contact you about your booking
  • To respond to enquiries
  • To improve our website

We do not use your data for automated profiling or sell it to third parties.

4. Data Storage

This website stores booking data in your browser's localStorage. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to a central server. It is used only to display your confirmation and allow you to add the appointment to your calendar.

5. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion
  • Object to processing

Email poomeigh503@gmail.com to exercise any right. We respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage strictly to maintain your booking flow and remember your popup preference. No advertising or tracking cookies are used. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

7. Security

This website is served over HTTPS. No card payment details are collected or stored.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance.
