About Blade & Bone

A Manchester barbershop built on craft, consistency and good conversation.

Est. 2016, Manchester

Built for the Craft

Blade & Bone opened on a quiet stretch of King Street in 2016 with one chair, one barber and a very clear idea of what a good barbershop should feel like. No attitude, no rush, no cutting corners. Just honest work done properly.

Eight years later we have three chairs, three exceptional barbers and a reputation that keeps clients coming back. The coffee is still the same brand. The chairs have been reupholstered twice. The standard hasn't changed once.

We're not a chain, not a franchise and not a trendy pop-up. We're a proper barbershop that happens to be good at what we do — and we intend to keep it that way.
