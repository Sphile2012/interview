Full Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

Blade & Bone Barbershop ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and store personal information when you visit our website or book an appointment with us.

Our contact details: 47 King Street, Manchester, M2 4LQ • 0161 834 7720 • hello@bladeandbonebarber.co.uk

2. What Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

2.1 Information You Give Us

  • Name, email address and phone number when you make a booking.
  • Any notes or preferences you provide during the booking process.
  • Messages sent through our contact form.

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Basic usage data such as pages visited and time spent on the site, collected via session cookies.
  • Your browser type and device type for the purpose of improving the website experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Manage your appointment and contact you about it.
  • Send appointment reminders or follow-up communications where you have consented.
  • Respond to enquiries submitted via our contact form.
  • Improve our website and services based on usage patterns.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract performance — to fulfil your booking and provide the service you've requested.
  • Legitimate interests — to improve our website and communicate effectively with clients.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required by law to retain or process your data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share data with trusted service providers (such as email delivery services) solely to operate our business, subject to strict confidentiality agreements.

6. Data Retention

We retain booking information for up to 2 years for business record-keeping purposes. Contact form data is retained for up to 6 months. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at hello@bladeandbonebarber.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Our website uses session cookies to maintain your booking flow (e.g. remembering your selected service between pages). These cookies are strictly necessary and do not track you across other websites. They expire when you close your browser.

We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss or disclosure. Our website is served over HTTPS. We do not store card payment details.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your data appropriately, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
